      📺 How to play Crypto TV Flip

      1. Install MetaMask (browser extension or app) and create a wallet.
      2. Switch to the test network. Click Connect Wallet — the site offers to add/switch to the right network automatically.
      3. Get free test ETH. On Sepolia, paste your address into a faucet (e.g. sepoliafaucet.com or cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia). On a local chain, test ETH is pre-funded — import a test key into MetaMask.
      4. Deposit ETH into the game from the panel under the TV.
      5. Create or join a room. Two players, equal bet. Max bet is shown by the form. Everything is pure ETH — no tokens, no swaps.
      6. Watch the TV. When both bets are in: 3‑2‑1‑FLIP — the coin is decided on‑chain by the block's randomness (prevrandao), settled instantly inside your transaction, and your screen shows WINNER 😄 or LOSER 😢.
      7. Winner takes the pot minus the 10% house fee. Withdraw your balance to your wallet anytime.

      Hotkeys: Space flip vs house / confirm bet · H/T pick side · +/− adjust stake · Esc close.

      The result is computed on‑chain and visible in the transaction on Etherscan. This is play‑money on a test network — never use real funds. (A Chainlink VRF randomness upgrade is planned for mainnet.)
