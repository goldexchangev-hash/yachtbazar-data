❔ How to play · Power · Bonus rounds

🎯 Goal. Land fish to win coins. Tap the tank to aim & fire, or use 🔥 Auto-fire + 🎯 Lock-on. Overall the game returns ~95% no matter which fish you shoot — most of it straight from your catches, plus a little through the bonus + jackpot rounds.

⚡ Power (×1–×2) is a MULTIPLIER, not your bet. It does not change your base bet. It multiplies both the cost and the payout of each shot by the same amount, so a power-2 shot costs 2× and pays 2× — your % return is essentially the same at every power. Higher power just lands big fish faster. (Capped at ×2 to keep the odds fair and consistent.)

💵 Base bet (the slider) sets your stake. Cost per shot = power × base bet. Bigger base bet → bigger bonus rounds (a bonus pays around 25× your base bet on average).

🎚 Shooting speed (Slow / Medium / Fast) sets your hold-to-fire pace. You can always click faster than that by tapping rapidly — go for it, it's fun and totally RTP-neutral (every shot still returns the same ~95%, you just spend faster). During bonus & boss rounds the free shots fire at your chosen pace so the round always plays out fairly — tapping won't speed those up.

🎁 Bonus rounds — rare, and they pay when you land the bonus creature:

  • ⚡ Feeding Frenzy — a burst of free shots.
  • 💰 Treasure Vault — free shots + a treasure chest.
  • 🌩 Lightning Storm — free shots where every kill chains lightning to nearby fish.
  • 🐉 Dragon Boss — fill the Jackpot meter (or land the Sea Dragon) to blast the dragon for the whole jackpot pool.

Bonus payouts are variable — you keep what you catch, so a lucky big fish pays big and a cold run pays small. They're pre-funded, so the odds stay fair.

      📺 How to play Crypto TV Flip

      1. Install MetaMask (browser extension or app) and create a wallet.
      2. Switch to the test network. Click Connect Wallet — the site offers to add/switch to the right network automatically.
      3. Get free test ETH. On Sepolia, the easiest faucet (gives the most) is sepolia-faucet.pk910.de ↗ — mine some Sepolia ETH to your address in the browser. (Alternatives: sepoliafaucet.com, cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia.) On a local chain, test ETH is pre-funded — import a test key into MetaMask.
      4. Deposit ETH into the game from the panel under the TV.
      5. Create or join a room. Two players, equal bet. Max bet is shown by the form. Everything is pure ETH — no tokens, no swaps.
      6. Watch the TV. When both bets are in: 3‑2‑1‑FLIP — the coin is decided on‑chain by the block's randomness (prevrandao), settled instantly inside your transaction, and your screen shows WINNER 😄 or LOSER 😢.
      7. Winner takes the pot minus the 3% house fee. Withdraw your balance to your wallet anytime.

      Hotkeys: Space flip vs house / confirm bet · H/T pick side · +/− adjust stake · Esc close.

      The result is computed on‑chain and visible in the transaction on Etherscan. This is play‑money on a test network — never use real funds. (A Chainlink VRF randomness upgrade is planned for mainnet.)
